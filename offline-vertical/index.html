Operating model

How I work when the answer is not already framed.

  • Name the real problem. Most first requests are approximations. I look for the product decision behind the ask.
  • Separate facts from generation. Models synthesize. Product systems decide, constrain, explain, and recover.
  • Design for day two. Adoption, latency, cost, governance, and workflow fit matter as much as launch.
  • Make tradeoffs inspectable. The team should know why a system behaves the way it does before scaling it.

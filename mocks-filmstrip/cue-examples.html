Cue studies

Signals that teach the user there is another frame.

These are not separate portfolio designs. They are motion affordances we can layer onto the filmstrip direction we pick.

A / edge pull

Show a sliver of the next world.

Current frame Next

Best for horizontal. It makes the right side feel alive without asking the recruiter to understand a new control.

B / motion instruction

Say what to do, then animate the direction.

Scroll down or right

Best for MacBook risk. If vertical wheel moves the horizontal track, this tells users their normal scroll behavior still works.

C / spiral attractor

Use a quiet kinetic mark to pull attention.

Most distinctive. It adds an authored, memorable cue, but should disappear after the first interaction so it does not become decoration.

Operating model

I start with the decision the team has to make.

  • Frame the decision. Most asks arrive as feature requests. I turn them into a decision about who needs to act, what evidence they need, and what tradeoff we can accept. Open Amplify framing
  • Keep facts out of prose. Verified data, policy, eligibility, and permissions get their own rails. The model writes inside those constraints. Open SproutRoute architecture
  • Prove the narrow version. A strong V1 makes adoption, latency, cost, and failure modes visible before the team adds surface area. Open Sprout Math scope
  • Leave a trail. Teams scale faster when they can inspect why a product ranked, blocked, cached, or generated something. Open AI evaluation gates

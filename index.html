How I operate

I start with the decision the company has to make.

01 / Frame

Find the real product decision.

Separate the stated feature request from the user action, business constraint, evidence requirement, and tradeoff the company can accept.

02 / Bound

Draw the deterministic and AI boundary.

Keep policy, eligibility, permissions, freshness, and safety floors in deterministic rails. Let AI synthesize and reason inside them.

03 / Prove

Make readiness inspectable.

Connect quality, safety, latency, cost, provenance, and business impact to an explicit owner and a PASS, WARN, or BLOCK decision.

04 / Scale

Design the operating mechanism.

Treat internal developers and operators as customers. Migration, onboarding, feedback, support, and unit economics decide whether a product scales.

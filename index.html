How I operate

From the customer outward.

  1. FrameFind the customer’s decision and the business result worth changing.
  2. BoundChoose the smallest useful product and be explicit about what stays out.
  3. ProveWatch people use it; measure the change and state the limits of the evidence.
  4. ScaleOwn rollout, adoption, reliability, and cost as part of the product.

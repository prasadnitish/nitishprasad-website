# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are recruiters, hiring managers, and product leaders evaluating Nitish Prasad for senior or principal product roles in AI systems, fintech, marketplaces, payments infrastructure, and technically complex 0-to-1 product spaces. They usually arrive with limited time, compare multiple candidates, and need to quickly understand scope, judgment, systems fluency, and evidence of impact.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to make Nitish legible as a product leader who can turn ambiguous technical and business problems into clear product direction, operating systems, and shipped outcomes. Success means a qualified recruiter or hiring manager leaves with a strong mental model of his work, remembers the experience, trusts the substance behind the design, and knows exactly which case study or contact path to follow next.

## Brand Personality

Precise, cinematic, systems-literate. The interface should feel confident and restrained, with enough editorial presence and motion to make the journey memorable without turning the portfolio into a decorative demo. The writing should be direct, concrete, and senior: no hype, no generic claims, no resume-dump cadence.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS cards, gradient hero tropes, beige editorial templates, terminal cosplay, over-numbered section scaffolds, dense resume timelines, dashboard cosplay, identical project grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section, and animation that blocks access to content. The site should not look like a template portfolio or an AI-generated landing page.

## Design Principles

1. Guide the recruiter through a sequence, not a document dump.
2. Reveal depth only when it clarifies judgment, scope, or impact.
3. Let technical fluency appear through product decisions, not jargon density.
4. Make the page memorable through pacing, spatial contrast, and purposeful motion.
5. Preserve substance for crawlers, ATS tools, AI assistants, keyboard users, and no-JavaScript readers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and essential controls. Support keyboard navigation, clear focus states, reduced-motion preferences, and mobile-friendly motion that avoids heavy scroll hijacking or fragile animation dependencies. Core content and navigation must remain available without JavaScript, and no important information should depend on an animation finishing.

Funding logic

Every capability carries a stop condition.

Fund

A shared capability when at least two workloads repeat the same control problem and the platform improves time, cost, risk, or evidence quality.

Federate

Domain logic, customer experience, prompts, and product-specific metrics remain with application teams under shared contracts.

Stop

If migration cost exceeds local benefit, teams bypass the control plane, or the capability cannot show measurable risk or unit-economic improvement.
